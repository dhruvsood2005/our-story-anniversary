We did not get everything right.
We got better.

Earlier this year, miscommunication and missing transparency caused hurt. We had moments when both of us needed more reassurance, clearer boundaries and a better way of listening.

We did not improve by pretending nothing happened. We learned how to be more open, how to say what we need directly, how to take accountability, and how to protect the relationship before a problem grows.

I learned that listening to your feelings matters more than immediately trying to fix them. You became more open, vulnerable and confident. Together, we became more honest and much closer emotionally.

TWO YEARS

A LETTER TO US

Two years later.

Shreya,

Two years ago, we were lying on an empty beach at The Entrance when you told me you loved me and asked me to be your boyfriend. I made you wait a few hours because I was afraid of agreeing to something as difficult as long distance without knowing whether I could do it properly. The funny thing is that we had already begun becoming us. The answer was sitting beside you, even while I was overthinking it.

When I think about our beginning, I do not only remember the obvious romantic moments. I remember grocery shopping, chicken wings in the car, getting ready together, public barbecues, being exhausted after hikes, your passenger princess behaviour, long conversations and your completely unnecessary reaction to my big toe. I remember how quickly being with you started to feel normal in the best possible way.

Our relationship has not only been those six weeks in Sydney. We have built most of it through screens, calls and the effort to stay present in each other’s lives from opposite sides of the world. We have had beautiful conversations and hard ones. We have misunderstood each other, hurt each other, learned, apologised and changed. I am proud that we did not settle for pretending everything was fine. We became more transparent, more vulnerable and much better at protecting what we have.

You have taught me that love needs consistency. You have taught me to listen to a feeling before trying to solve it, and to reassure you clearly instead of assuming you already know what I mean. I hope I have helped you feel safer being direct, honest and fully yourself with me. I love the strength and self respect you have built, and I love how much closer we have become because we are willing to have the conversations that actually matter.

No one understands me in quite the same way you do. Our deep conversations are still my favourite part of us. I miss the physical things too: holding you, teasing you in person, kissing you, cuddling, going on drives and being close to you in every way. But what I miss underneath all of that is the ease of sharing a life beside you.

I cannot promise that we will never struggle again. I can promise that I want to keep listening, speaking honestly, respecting our boundaries and choosing the relationship with you. I want the ordinary future as much as the exciting one: cooking, working, dates, trips, adventures, making love, and eventually building a home together once our careers let us be in the same place.

Happy two years, love. We have already carried this across an ocean. Soon, I get to hold you again.

I love you so much.
Dhruv
